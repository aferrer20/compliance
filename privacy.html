How we handle your dossier

This notice covers information submitted through this portal to Chief Negotiators LLC and Strategic Supply Partners ("we"). It sits alongside any confidentiality agreement you have with us.

What we collect

The answers and documents in your dossier, including company, ownership, director, banking and transaction details, and the names and contact details of the people you list. We also record the time taken to complete the dossier, your browser type and the IP address it was submitted from, to protect against fraud.

Why we use it

  • Counterparty due diligence: verifying identity, ownership, banking and authenticity.
  • Sanctions, export-control and anti-money-laundering screening.
  • Meeting OEM and NVIDIA end-user and compliance requirements for a transaction you have asked us to support.
  • Keeping the records that the law and our OEM relationships require.

Who sees it

Only the named reviewers on our vetting desk. We share information with an OEM, NVIDIA, a bank or an authority only where the transaction or the law requires it, and never with another counterparty without your agreement. Our processors (cloud hosting, database and email delivery) handle it under contract and only on our instructions.

How it is protected

Transmission is encrypted. Dossiers are stored in an access-controlled database restricted to named reviewers. Documents are held in a private store and opened only through short-lived links. Every review action is logged.

How long we keep it

For as long as you are an approved counterparty, then for five years after the relationship or the last related transaction ends, in line with U.S. export-control record-keeping requirements. Declined dossiers are kept for the same period so that we can recognise repeat attempts.

Your choices

You may ask to see, correct or update the information we hold about you, or ask questions about this notice, at amanda@thechiefnegotiators.com. Where we must keep a record by law, we will tell you.
